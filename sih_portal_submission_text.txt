FIELD 3: ABSTRACT / SUMMARY (Max 10000 Characters)
1. The Core Problem
Government tertiary hospitals and apex institutes like AIIA and AIIMS handle 4,000 to 10,000 outpatients every day. A global BMJ Open study places India's average primary care consultation at just 2 minutes. Within this narrow window, a doctor must take patient history across language barriers, decipher crumpled handwritten paper records, conduct physical examinations, formulate diagnoses, and prescribe. In Ayurvedic OPDs, holistic diagnosis requires comprehensive Trividha, Ashtavidha, and Dashavidha Pariksha (assessing Prakriti, Agni, Koshtha, and Ahara-Vihara). Doing this manually under intense OPD rush is virtually impossible, forcing clinicians to abbreviate case-taking. Furthermore, while the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) has built national digital health highways, the first-mile intake remains completely analog and broken.

2. The Proposed Solution: Project Samanvaya
Project Samanvaya (Tagline: Collect. Connect. Cure.) is an AI-powered clinical case-taking and document digitization platform designed specifically for high-throughput Indian public hospitals and Ayush healthcare centers. Placed at OPD waiting bays, Samanvaya acts as an intelligent digital triage resident. Before entering the consultation room, patients independently build a complete medical history through conversational voice in their native language or intuitive touch cards, while an optical scanner digitizes prior medical records. A validated, chronologically structured clinical case sheet is delivered to the physician's screen before the patient walks in.

3. What It Does (Core Modules)
- Multilingual Voice & Touch Intake: Guided symptom elicitation in 22 Indian regional languages using speech recognition tuned for noisy hospital environments, combined with high-contrast visual touch cards.
- Comprehensive Ayush Mode: Dedicated Dashavidha Pariksha intake capturing Agni, Koshtha, Prakriti, and lifestyle patterns through simple vernacular analogies.
- 4-Tier Medical Document Digitization: Scans handwritten doctor prescriptions, lab reports, and discharge summaries, extracts clinical entities, builds a chronological medical timeline, and flags out-of-range lab values.
- Physician-Ready Case Summary: Synthesizes spoken history and scanned reports into standard clinical format (Chief Complaint, HPI via SOCRATES, Past History, Drug/Allergy, Family, ROS) for fast one-click doctor confirmation.
- Red-Flag Emergency Sentinel: Instantly detects life-threatening emergency symptoms (acute cardiac pain, stroke signs, severe dyspnea) and triggers immediate nurse alerts, bypassing routine queues.
- ABDM & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance: Seamless sign-in via ABHA ID, audio-guided consent, and automatic session data wipe on kiosk terminals.

4. Where Samanvaya Goes Further (Key Differentiators)
- Autonomous Floating AI Assistant: An ambient voice-guided copilot that auto-navigates portals, helps illiterate patients complete intake hands-free, and assists doctors.
- Govt Scheme Engine & Jan Aushadhi Savings: Identifies eligible government welfare schemes (PM-JAY, Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi) and recommends generic substitutes calculating up to 80%+ savings.
- Clinical Safety Net: Herb-drug interaction checker screening concurrent Ayush and Allopathic medications, alongside WHO AWaRe antibiotic stewardship audits.
- Dual-Branch AI Architecture: Combines a 120B+ general reasoning model with a 70B+ dedicated medical model (Llama-3-Med) backed by deterministic safety guardrails.
- Working Live Prototype: Already fully developed and deployed with complete patient, physician, and admin interfaces.

5. Measured Impact
Reduces doctor documentation time by over 80% (from 5 minutes of repetitive questioning to a 60-second review), accelerates OPD throughput by 4x to 5x, and ensures thorough, dignified healthcare for every citizen.


FIELD 4: IDEA DESCRIPTION (Max 50000 Characters)
1. PROBLEM DEFINITION & THE OPD BOTTLENECK
- Severe Consultation Deficit: Indian government tertiary OPDs register 4,000-10,000 patients daily. Average doctor-patient consultation time is just 2 minutes (BMJ Open, 2017).
- Unstructured Paper Burden: Patients carry fragmented, handwritten paper prescriptions and laboratory printouts, forcing doctors to waste precious minutes manually sorting documents.
- Ayush Documentation Crisis: Comprehensive Ayurvedic intake (Dashavidha, Ashtavidha, Prakriti, Agni, and Koshtha Pariksha) cannot be captured in 2 minutes, forcing doctors to abbreviate classical case-taking.
- The First-Mile ABDM Gap: India has digital health infrastructure (ABHA, HIE-CM), but lacks an automated point-of-entry intake system to digitize patient history before the doctor consultation.


2. THE PROPOSED SOLUTION: UNIFIED 3-PORTAL ECOSYSTEM
Project Samanvaya shifts clinical history-taking and document structuring to the waiting area via three connected portals:
1. Patient OPD Kiosk & Mobile PWA: Touchscreen kiosk and QR-accessible mobile app for conversational voice/touch intake in 22 languages and rapid optical document scanning.
2. Physician Consultation Desk: Clean doctor workstation showing the pre-generated structured case summary, timeline, flagged lab values, and red-flag alerts for rapid 60-second review and confirmation.
3. Diagnostic & Admin Oversight Portal: Centralized dashboard for queue triage management, emergency tracking, and hospital analytics.


3. CORE FUNCTIONAL MODULES (SIH26047 COMPLIANCE)
- Module A - Conversational Multimodal History Engine:
  * Dual-mode voice and touch input powered by Indian-language ASR (Bhashini / AI4Bharat) with noise suppression for hospital halls.
  * Adaptive questioning following the SOCRATES framework (Site, Onset, Character, Radiation, Associations, Timing, Exacerbating factors, Severity).
  * Dedicated Ayush Pariksha assessing Agni, Koshtha, and Ahara-Vihara using accessible vernacular analogies.
  * Autonomous red-flag triage immediately escalating cardiac, stroke, or severe respiratory symptoms to nursing staff.

- Module B - Medical Document Digitization & Intelligence:
  * 4-Tier OCR engine reading printed and handwritten Indian doctor prescriptions, lab slips, and discharge summaries.
  * Automatically extracts diagnoses, drugs, dosages, and lab parameters into a chronological medical timeline.
  * Highlights abnormal/out-of-range clinical values in bold visual alerts.

- Module C - Structured Clinical Summary Generator:
  * Assembles data into standardized format: Chief Complaint, HPI, Past History, Drugs/Allergies, Family/Social History, and ROS.
  * Doctor-in-the-loop: The physician retains full authority to accept, modify, or reject AI drafts in seconds.
  * Bilingual output: Medical English for physicians, native-language voice summary for patients.

- Module D - Consent, Privacy & ABDM Integration:
  * Full ABHA ID integration and FHIR R4 schema compliance.
  * DPDP Act 2023 aligned: Audio-guided explicit consent and automatic ephemeral session memory wipe on kiosk completion.


4. KEY INNOVATIONS: WHERE SAMANVAYA GOES FURTHER
- Autonomous Floating AI Assistant: Ambient voice-guided copilot navigating the app hands-free for low-literacy patients.
- Government Scheme & Jan Aushadhi Engine: Auto-matches patient profiles against PM-JAY and Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi, maps branded drugs to Jan Aushadhi generic equivalents, and calculates 80%+ cost savings.
- Herb-Drug Interaction & Safety Sentinel: Screens concurrent Ayurvedic herbs and Allopathic medications for safety contraindications (e.g., Guggulu with blood thinners), alongside WHO AWaRe antibiotic audits.
- Dual-Branch AI Core: Pairs a 120B+ general reasoning model with a 70B+ medically fine-tuned model (Llama-3-Med) with deterministic safety guardrails.
- Hybrid Edge-Cloud Architecture: Lightweight edge ASR and document caching run locally on kiosk hardware, ensuring uninterrupted intake even during hospital internet outages.


5. 5-STEP END-TO-END PATIENT JOURNEY
Step 1 - Identify: Patient selects language, scans ABHA QR / enters mobile number, and confirms audio-guided consent.
Step 2 - Converse: Conversational voice/touch interview captures symptoms via SOCRATES and Ayush Pariksha; red flags trigger instant priority triage.
Step 3 - Scan: Patient uploads paper records; 4-tier OCR digitizes, chronologically timelines, and flags abnormal lab markers.
Step 4 - Summarize & Route: AI synthesizes data into a structured FHIR clinical draft, pushes to doctor screen, and prints queue token while wiping local kiosk memory.
Step 5 - Consult: Doctor reviews pre-structured history in under 60 seconds, confirms findings, and devotes the consultation to examination and counseling.


6. TECHNICAL STACK & SPECIFICATIONS
- Frontend: React 19, TypeScript, Next.js, TailwindCSS (High-contrast, touch-optimized, accessibility-first).
- Backend: Python 3.11, FastAPI (Asynchronous high-concurrency), Pydantic, Celery with Redis.
- AI & Vision: Bhashini Indic Speech Suite, Tesseract + TrOCR for handwriting, Dual LLM Core (120B+ general / 70B+ clinical), OpenCV image pre-processing.
- Standards & Security: FHIR R4, SNOMED-CT, ICD-10, Ayush NAMASTE terminology, AES-256 encryption, TLS 1.3, DPDP Act 2023.


7. CONCLUSION & IMPACT
Project Samanvaya eliminates the 2-minute OPD documentation bottleneck. By uniting vernacular voice AI, document vision, Ayush diagnostic rigor, and national ABDM highways, Samanvaya delivers 5x patient throughput while restoring empathy and clinical depth to Indian public healthcare.
